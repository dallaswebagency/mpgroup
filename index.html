Who we are

A family business that grew by doing the job right.

MP Group was founded as a family business in 1994 and incorporated in Alberta in 2003. Our rapid growth comes from excellent caretaking services, a good reputation and quick response to emergencies.

Our success would not be possible without qualified, well trained personnel. Our commitment to health and safety through ACSA membership contributes to high employee retention, and ISSA membership helps us equip our team with leading edge technologies, techniques and processes.
